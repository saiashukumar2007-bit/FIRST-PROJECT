ABOUT PUNJAB🌾🚜🔫

  • 🌟 The Meaning of Punjab (The Naming): The word Punjab comes from two Persian words: "Punj" means five, and "Ab" means water. Together, Punjab means the "Land of Five Rivers." These five historic rivers are the Jhelum, Chenab, Ravi, Beas, and Sutlej.
  • 📜 Ancient History: Punjab has a deep and rich history. Thousands of years ago, it was the heart of the Indus Valley Civilization, one of the oldest human civilizations in the world. Because of its location, Punjab was the main gateway into India. Great kings and conquerors passed through Punjab, including Alexander the Great. It is also the birthplace of Sikhism, founded by Guru Nanak Dev Ji in the late 15th century. Later, in the early 1800s, Maharaja Ranjit Singh united the region into a powerful and prosperous Sikh Empire.
  • 📅 When Punjab Was Born (Modern History): The Punjab we know today was born through two major splits in history: • 1947 (The Great Partition): When India became independent, the old region of Punjab was divided into two parts. The western part became part of Pakistan, and the eastern part stayed with India. • November 1, 1966 (Modern Punjab): This is the official birthday of the modern Indian state of Punjab. On this day, the Indian government divided the region based on language. The Punjabi-speaking areas became the state of Punjab, while the Hindi-speaking areas became Haryana and Himachal Pradesh.
